Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we collect the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and allowing you to manage your records. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all your personal data and expense records will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces for the interface. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the site. No personal expense data is shared with Google or any other third party.

We do not integrate with analytics platforms, advertising networks, or social media trackers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the app or contact us at the email address associated with your account. We will respond as promptly as possible.
